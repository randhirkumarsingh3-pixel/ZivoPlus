About Us

Smarter Hotel Bookings.
Better Travel Experiences.

Building a digital platform to simplify hotel discovery, accommodation reservations, and travel distribution.

ZivoPlus is an emerging hotel booking and travel technology platform being developed by Zivo Technologies Solutions Private Limited in India. Our vision is to simplify hotel discovery, accommodation reservations, and travel distribution through a convenient digital experience.

We aim to serve individual travellers, corporate customers, travel agents, and accommodation distribution partners. Our platform is currently under development as we prepare for launch and explore opportunities to collaborate with established travel and hospitality businesses.

Our goal is to create a trustworthy, user-friendly booking experience that brings together travellers and accommodation providers through modern technology and thoughtful design.
