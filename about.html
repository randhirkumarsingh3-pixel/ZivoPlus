About ZivoPlus

Smarter Hotel Bookings. Better Travel Experiences.

Who We Are

ZivoPlus is an emerging hotel booking and travel technology platform being developed by Zivo Technologies Solutions Private Limited in India. Our vision is to simplify hotel discovery, accommodation reservations, and travel distribution through a convenient digital experience.

Our Vision

We aim to create a trustworthy, user-friendly platform that connects travellers with accommodation options for leisure and business travel. Our goal is to bring together modern technology and thoughtful design to make hotel booking more accessible and convenient.

Who We Serve

ZivoPlus is being designed to serve a broad range of customers and partners:

  • Individual Travellers — People looking for hotels and accommodation for personal or holiday travel.
  • Corporate Customers — Businesses seeking accommodation solutions for employee travel and corporate bookings.
  • Travel Agents — Travel professionals who may benefit from hotel distribution and booking tools.
  • Distribution Partners — Travel technology companies and accommodation providers interested in collaboration.

Current Status

Our platform is currently under development as we prepare for launch and explore opportunities to collaborate with established travel and hospitality businesses. The ZivoPlus website serves as our online business presence while we build the platform.

Booking functionality and third-party integrations are not yet available. We are committed to transparent communication about our development progress and launch timeline.
